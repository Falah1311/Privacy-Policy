# SAYF — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 28 August 2026**
**Publisher: Wahaj Studio Games — wahajstudiogames@gmail.com**

SAYF is a single-player idle role-playing game. This policy explains what
the game handles, what leaves your device, and what you can do about it.

> This is a working draft written to match what the game actually does. Have
> it reviewed before publishing if you want legal certainty in a particular
> country.

## What we collect ourselves

Nothing. SAYF has no accounts, no login, no analytics and no servers of our
own. Your progress — levels, stages, gold, jawhar, equipment, relics — is
written to a save file on your own device and never sent anywhere. Deleting
the game, or using RESET SAVE in the settings, erases it permanently. We
cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

## What third parties collect

Two Google services run inside the game.

**Google AdMob** serves the optional rewarded ads — the ones you choose to
watch in exchange for jawhar, dinars or a doubled chest. To do that, AdMob
may collect your device's advertising identifier, coarse location derived
from your IP address, and information about the ad you were shown. It is
governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game shows
Google's consent form before the first ad request, and the choice can be
changed at any time from **Settings → Ad Privacy Choices**. Players who buy
The Silent Souk see no ads at all.

**Google Play Billing** processes in-app purchases. Payment details are
handled entirely by Google and are never seen by us; the game receives only
a token confirming that a purchase was made, which it uses to deliver the
goods and to restore permanent purchases on a new device.

## Children

SAYF is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Your choices

- Reset the advertising identifier, or opt out of personalised ads, in your
  Android settings.
- Change or withdraw ad consent in **Settings → Ad Privacy Choices** (shown
  where consent is required).
- Remove ads entirely by purchasing The Silent Souk.
- Erase everything the game stores by using RESET SAVE, or by uninstalling.
